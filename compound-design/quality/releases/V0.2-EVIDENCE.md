# Compound Design v0.2 — Evidence

Release type: POC evolution  
Theme: **separate quality of construction from quality of evidence**

## Why this version exists

v0.1 established a coherent resource architecture. Its main weakness was epistemic: a single readiness score risked being read as proof.

v0.2 makes the framework harder to fool.

## Changed

### `cd` 0.2.0
- smallest-sufficient stage routing;
- explicit user-facing AI branch;
- instructions/data/tools/actions boundary;
- CDQI/CEL separation.

### `jakub` 0.2.0
- explicit primary job and non-goals;
- severity model;
- ordered review sequence;
- evidence-bearing output contract.

### `emi` 0.2.0
- explicit primary job and non-goals;
- motion decision sequence before animation;
- severity model;
- clearer routing away from structural/AI concerns.

### `cd-quality-gate` 0.2.0
- construction score separated from evidence;
- CEL E0–E4;
- meta-evaluation of the evaluator;
- E2 default for approval.

### `cd-resource-lab` 0.2.0
- reproduce → hypothesis → eval → minimal change;
- holdout/falsification check;
- separate CDQI/CEL deltas.

### New: `cd-ai-interaction-review` 0.1.0
Six bounded gates:
- AI Fit
- Appropriate Reliance
- Legibility
- Human Control
- Proportional Autonomy
- Evolution

This is a skill, not another persona/agent.
